About the author
JR
J. R. Caldera
Archaeological puzzle thriller author
J.R. Caldera is a storyteller with a deep passion for ancient civilizations, forgotten knowledge, and the thrilling question of what happens when the past refuses to stay buried.

With The Cartographer of Giza, he turns from ancient conspiracies and buried gods to something closer to home: memory itself. What if the one gift that makes you extraordinary — a mind that forgets nothing — became the very thing a living pyramid could use against you?

When he's not writing, J.R. enjoys exploring old maps, learning about lost technologies, and imagining what secrets our ancestors might still be trying to tell us. He loves connecting with readers and always appreciates hearing which corridor, chamber, or twist stayed with them longest.
